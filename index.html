Some Important Travel Terms

Itinerary
A detailed plan or schedule of your trip, including destinations, activities, and accommodations.
All-Inclusive
A type of vacation package that includes accommodations, meals, drinks, and various activities for a single price.
Travel Insurance
A type of insurance that covers unexpected events during your trip, such as medical emergencies, trip cancellations, or lost luggage.
Visa
An official document or stamp in your passport that allows you to enter and stay in a foreign country for a specified period.
Layover
A short stop or break between connecting flights during a journey.
